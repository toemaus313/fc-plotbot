# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: October 30, 2025**

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the Fleet Carrier Route Plotter bot ("the Bot", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our Discord bot service.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 User-Provided Information
When you use the Bot's `/fcroute` command, you provide:
- **System Names**: Starting and destination system names for Elite Dangerous
- **Capacity Information**: Current cargo capacity used (in tons)
- **Fuel Information**: Current tritium fuel on board (in tons)

### 2.2 Automatically Collected Information
The Bot may process:
- **Discord User ID**: To respond to your commands
- **Server ID**: To operate within Discord servers
- **Command Timestamps**: For logging and debugging purposes

### 2.3 Information We Do NOT Collect
- We do NOT collect or store personal information
- We do NOT track your Discord activity beyond command usage
- We do NOT collect message content outside of bot commands
- We do NOT share your data with third parties for marketing

## 3. How We Use Information

### 3.1 Primary Purpose
Information you provide is used solely to:
- Process your route calculation request
- Send data to the Spansh.co.uk API for route computation
- Display route results back to you in Discord

### 3.2 Technical Operations
We may use data for:
- **Error Logging**: To diagnose and fix technical issues
- **Performance Monitoring**: To ensure the Bot operates correctly
- **Service Improvement**: To enhance Bot functionality

## 4. Data Storage and Retention

### 4.1 Temporary Processing
- Route data is processed in real-time and is NOT permanently stored
- Information is only held in memory during command execution
- Data is cleared once the route response is delivered

### 4.2 Logs
- Error logs may contain command parameters for debugging purposes
- Logs are retained only as long as necessary for troubleshooting
- Logs do not contain sensitive personal information

## 5. Data Sharing

### 5.1 Third-Party Services
Your route data is sent to:
- **Spansh.co.uk API**: To perform route calculations
  - See Spansh's privacy practices at https://spansh.co.uk

### 5.2 Discord
The Bot operates through Discord's platform:
- Discord's Privacy Policy applies: https://discord.com/privacy
- Discord may process data according to their policies

### 5.3 No Data Sales
We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your data to third parties.

## 6. Data Security

### 6.1 Security Measures
We implement reasonable security measures to protect data during processing, including:
- Secure communication with external APIs
- Limited data retention
- Access controls on bot infrastructure

### 6.2 Limitations
No method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of your data.

## 7. Your Rights

### 7.1 Data Access
Since we don't permanently store your data, there is minimal data to access. Route information is temporary and discarded after processing.

### 7.2 Data Deletion
You can stop using the Bot at any time. Since data is not permanently stored, discontinuing use effectively removes your data.

### 7.3 Opt-Out
You may opt-out of the Bot's services by:
- Not using the `/fcroute` command
- Removing the Bot from your Discord server
- Contacting us to block your access

## 8. Children's Privacy

The Bot is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. Discord's Terms of Service require users to be 13 or older.

## 9. International Users

The Bot may be hosted in various locations. By using the Bot, you consent to the transfer of your data to these locations.

## 10. Changes to Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be effective when posted. Continued use after changes indicates acceptance of the updated policy.

## 11. Third-Party Links

The Bot may provide links to Spansh.co.uk. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of external sites.

## 12. Contact Information

For questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact the bot developer:
- Via Discord support server (if available)
- Through the bot's GitHub repository (if public)

## 13. Compliance

We aim to comply with applicable data protection laws, including:
- General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) for EU users
- California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) for California users
- Other applicable regional privacy laws

## 14. Discord Bot Permissions

The Bot requires the following Discord permissions:
- **Send Messages**: To respond with route information
- **Use Slash Commands**: To receive `/fcroute` commands
- **Embed Links**: To display formatted route results

We only use permissions necessary for Bot functionality.
